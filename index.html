About the Practice

Architecture with intention.

Ntalasharchitects is a Lusaka-based studio committed to contextual design — buildings that respond to climate, culture, and community. We partner with clients from first sketch to final handover, ensuring every decision serves both beauty and utility.

Our approach is collaborative, rigorous, and rooted in the belief that exceptional architecture is accessible — not a luxury reserved for the few.
